Keeping People in Their Homes: the impact of emergency rental assistance on evictions

A neighborhood-level evaluation of whether $46.6 billion in emergency rental assistance reduced eviction filings, across 700+ counties and 12,000+ census tracts in 22 states.

Washington eviction filings, 2016–2026

Over the past 36 months, 1 in 45 renter households faced an eviction filing, roughly 49% above the pre-pandemic normal. Seven of 39 counties exceed the state rate.
