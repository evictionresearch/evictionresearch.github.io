# MEMO — "5 notices per filing": what's verifiable (fetched 2026-07-14)

## The recalled 5:1 is actually INFORMAL:FORMAL evictions, not notices:filings
- ORIGIN: Gromis & Desmond 2021, Cityscape 23(2):279-289 (2017 AHS): "The ratio of informal-to-formal evictions for the United States (5.5 informal evictions for every formal eviction)" — from forced moves: informal 72.3%, formal 13.1% (72.3/13.1=5.5). Milwaukee prior: 2:1; NYC/Newark 2.5:1 (p. 281). Authors caution AHS undercounts FORMAL evictions ("0.8 percent ... 65 percent lower than ... court records (2.3 percent)"), inflating the 5.5. https://www.huduser.gov/portal/periodicals/cityscpe/vol23num2/ch15.pdf (URL spelling "cityscpe" correct)
- POPULARIZER: New America (Zainulbhai & Daly, 1/20/2022): "a ratio of 5.5 informal evictions for every formal eviction." https://www.newamerica.org/future-land-housing/reports/informal-evictions-measuring-housing-displacement-outside-the-courtroom/
- CLOSEST TO RECALLED WORDING: Oregon Law Center testimony (2025): "for every formal eviction case, 2 to 5 times as many people face informal evictions, where landlord notices push the tenant out before any court paperwork is filed." https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2025r1/Downloads/CommitteeMeetingDocument/307316
- BAHFA/ABAG (July 2025): "informal evictions are two to three times more common than court evictions" (p.5); Bay Area filings 21,767 (7/2023-6/2024). https://abag.ca.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2025-07/BAHFA_Evictions-Nine-County-Report-07-27-25.pdf
- NO published "~5 notices per filing" sentence exists (exhaustive search).

## True notice-vs-filing counts
- WASHINGTON (only statewide notice census; ≈4-6:1 w/ era caveat): E2SSB 5160 §7(4) (2021, expired 7/1/2023) required pay-or-vacate notices sent to dispute resolution centers. "158,251 ERPP notices received statewide during the two-year pilot," ">7,000 per month"; "Prior to the ERPP, information on the statewide volume of Pay or Vacate notices ... was not collected" (Resolution WA p.2, https://www.resolutionwa.org/_files/ugd/eb99c8_c650f512a7dc42d98942579b0c224006.pdf); AOC report: 51,022 notices 7/2021-5/2022; volume "significantly higher ... than anyone understood" (pp.16, 26, https://www.courts.wa.gov/newsinfo/pdf/ERPP%20Full%20Legislative%20Report%20to%20the%20Legislature.pdf). Filings yardstick (Cascade PBS 11/28/2023): 2019 >15,000; normal ~20,000/yr; Oct 2023 >2,000/mo. COMPUTED: >7,000/mo notices vs 1,250-1,667/mo filings ≈ 4.2-5.6:1 (eras differ; notices counted during suppressed-filing years; serial monthly notices common per Bellevue CRC report).
- LOS ANGELES (ongoing registry; ≥2:1 hard floor): LAHD received 290,560 notices Feb 2023-Apr 2026; 93% nonpayment; 89% 3-day (LA Controller, https://controller.lacity.gov/landings/evictions). CY2023: 77,049 city notices (archive) vs ~45,000 COUNTYWIDE UD filings (Judicial Council CSR Table 4a: 44,216 FY22-23; 46,825 FY23-24; 41,059 FY24-25) ≈ 1.7; rate basis ≈2.0. City numerator vs county denominator -> true within-city ratio higher. No LA source publishes a ratio.
- SAN FRANCISCO — RUNS THE OTHER WAY (never cite for notices>filings): Rent Board notices 797 (23-24), 1,033 (24-25), 1,488 (25-26) vs SF UD filings 2,781 (FY23-24), 3,436 (FY24-25) — filings ≈3.3-3.5x notices, because "This filing requirement does not apply to evictions based on non-payment of rent" (sf.gov). BLA 2014 said Rent Board stats "undercount total evictions" (pp.2,5, https://sfbos.archive.sf.gov/sites/default/files/FileCenter/Documents/48669-BLA%20Unlawful%20Detainers%20041514.pdf).
- PHILADELPHIA (mandatory pre-filing diversion): filing within 6 months = 34.7% (Phase 2)/55.6% (Phase 3) of diversion cases -> ≈2.9/1.8 applications per filing; 7.4% with rental assistance (~13.5:1) (Reinvestment Fund 11/2023 p.5, https://www.reinvestment.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Eviction-Diversion-Brief-11.29.23.pdf). Counts enrollments, not notices.
- Losing Home 2018: court files only; contains NO notice counts (don't cite for this).
- Colorado: SB24-064 collects filings only.

## Adjacent conflatables
- Filings != households: 3.6M filings/yr vs ~2.7M households -> ≈1.33 filings/threatened household; PNAS "threatened" = received a FILING (Gromis et al. 2022).
- Lundberg & Donnelly 2019: completed evictions by age 15 (14.8% urban-born children) — no notice item.
- Milwaukee MARS: informal 48% vs formal 24% of forced moves = the 2:1 (Desmond, IRP Fast Focus 22-2015 p.3).
- ERN Oregon page already frames: "Notices are not administratively collected and this study only examines cases that went to court."

## Defensible public statement
Filings are a floor: where notices are actually counted they outrun filings ~2:1 (LA, hard floor) to ~4-6:1 (WA 2021-23 pilot, era caveat); and nationally an estimated 5.5 INFORMAL evictions occur for every formal one (Gromis & Desmond 2021) — a displacement ratio, not a notice count.
