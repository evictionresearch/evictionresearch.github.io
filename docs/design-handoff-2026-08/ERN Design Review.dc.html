One red, two voices — and the blues get real jobs.

The darker red exists for exactly one reason (2a) — and there's a principled way to keep #F9322B visibly the brand red anyway. The working palette (2b) folds in the neighborhood R-package colors as the data-viz layer, which is where the blues earn their keep.

2a The red, resolved why #CC2118 exists · how #F9322B stays the star
#19222C
#223754
#F9322B
#CC2118

The good reason: Berkeley requires WCAG 2.1 AA on every ERN site (dap.berkeley.edu · full deadline April 26, 2027). AA demands 4.5:1 contrast for body-size text — #F9322B measures 3.8:1 on white and fails. #CC2118 is not a fourth brand color; it is the brand red pitched down just far enough to read (5.5:1 on white, 4.7:1 on the tint). Links, buttons, kickers, small labels — anything red you can read at body size — wear #CC2118 so the site stays legal and legible.

23,913 #F9322B at display scale — legal

But here's the escape hatch WCAG itself provides: large text (24px+ regular, 19px+ bold) only needs 3:1 — and #F9322B passes at 3.8:1. So the brand red can legally carry display-scale type: hero emphasis words, big stat numbers, section-title ems. I recommend amending BRAND.md's "never as text" to "never at body scale" — that one line brings #F9322B back to the surface everywhere it matters visually, with zero accessibility cost.

Washington eviction filings.

For the past 33 months, 1 in 47 renter households in Washington faced an eviction filing, roughly 45% above the pre-pandemic normal, with no return toward that normal in sight.

In short

→It's statewide, not a Seattle story. The highest filing rates are in suburban and rural counties: Clark, Pierce, Snohomish, Thurston, Spokane.

→Most filings are about cash flow, not bad faith. Predominantly nonpayment, by households one setback from falling behind.

→It's fixable, and prevention is the cheapest option. Filings rise and fall with policy.

Salvage

Display-scale numerals (already living on as the action list's 01/02); density-contrast charts — focal series solid, context muted (the WA county chart's red-vs-muted-blue lines are this idea, matured); Berkeley co-branding (pending the official lockup from Berkeley brand services).

Dead — and why

JetBrains Mono kickers break the one-typeface rule (Inter only, per dubois_lineage.md hard constraint #1). Roboto/Open Sans stack superseded 2026-04-29. B&W photography violates the imagery rule ("never black-and-white"). Keep it shelved.

Verdict

Washington is the house style. The home page should become its national edition.

This isn't a coin flip between three peers. The three pages are one lineage at three ages: the home page froze at the template era, HUD-ERA proved the v5b language in production, and Washington finished the thought — WCAG-safe palette, the Figure system, plain-language claims, data-forward hero. The canonical brand guide already codifies Washington's choices.

So the decision is not "which design wins" but "finish the migration": retire the Bootstrap hero, patch the two stale stylesheets, and rebuild the home page in the Washington language — telling the national story the way state profiles tell theirs.

The settled language — one spec

Type — Inter only. 200 display · 400 body · 500 nav · 600 em/buttons · 700 claims · 800 figure numbers. ss01 + cv11 on.
Scale — 11 · 13 · 16 · 18 · 22 · 30 fixed + clamp() display. Nothing in between.
Red, readable — #CC2118 text/links/buttons · #B01D16 hover. Never #F9322B as type.
Red, ink — #F9322B for markers, rules, map fills, borders only.
Ground — white paper · tint rgba(232,238,244,.45) bands · navy #19222C reserved for footer/interstitial.
Chrome — sticky white header (blur), hairline rules rgba(25,34,44,.10/.18), 4px-radius buttons with → tail.
Rhetoric — numbered Figures, claim-sentence titles, 1–2 line interpretive descriptions, numbers-not-adjectives.
Muted — #586573 on light · #8BA3BE on dark only.
