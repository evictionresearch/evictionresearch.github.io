Eviction help & tenant resources

Legal aid, rental assistance, and housing counseling for tenants facing eviction — organized by state. Listings cover regions where the Eviction Research Network has conducted active research.

If you need help right now Dial 2-1-1 from any phone for immediate connection to rental assistance, shelter, and tenant-legal services in your area. If you've received court papers, contact a legal aid program today — response deadlines are often 5–10 days.
