U.S. Eviction Data Directory

Every state's best available eviction data.

No federal agency counts evictions, so the public record is scattered across state courts, legal aid organizations, universities, and research groups. This page lists the best public source of eviction data we know of in each state and the District of Columbia. Where ERN has built a profile, we link our own work. Where another group already publishes reliable data, we link theirs and credit them. Every link was checked on August 13, 2026.

Five states have no public eviction data that we can find. In Alabama, Iowa, Kansas, Mississippi, and South Dakota, eviction filings exist in court records but no court, agency, university, or research group publishes them. If a public source is missing from this list, or a link has broken, email evictions@berkeley.edu.
