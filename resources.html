Eviction help, by state

Legal aid, rent assistance, and housing counseling for every U.S. state plus Washington, D.C. Tap a state to see local programs — with deeper coverage for the states where the Eviction Research Network conducts active research.

Need help right now?

Dial 2-1-1 from any phone to reach a trained specialist who can connect you to rent assistance, shelter, and tenant-legal referrals in your area — any state, any time.

Call 2-1-1 National rent-relief directory

Got court papers? Contact a legal-aid program the same day. Response deadlines are often 5–10 days and missing them can lead to a default judgment.

Find help in your state

All 50 states plus Washington, D.C. Listings include the state’s Legal Services Corporation grantee, statewide rent and utility programs, and—for larger states—major city and county programs. Choose your state below or jump from the index.

National resources

If you need to compare programs across states, or if your local listing above is outdated, the federal Consumer Financial Protection Bureau maintains a national directory of rent and utility assistance programs. 2-1-1 is a free, confidential helpline that connects callers in any U.S. state to local social services, including housing and homelessness prevention.
