# MEMO — Who Collects Eviction Data (verified inventory, fetched 2026-07-14)

## Anchors
- CCDI/LSC: Civil Court Data Initiative + "LSC Eviction Tracker" — 1,250 counties/municipalities, 30 states/territories, 30M+ records since 2016; eviction + debt + guardianship. ACTIVE ("Data last updated July 13, 2026"). January Advisors = advisory (Jeff Richman on team page; also Hepburn, Stanford LDL, NCSC, Pew). https://civilcourtdata.lsc.gov/ ; /data/eviction ; /about/team/
- Evicted in Oregon — PSU Center for Urban Studies, led by Dr. Lisa K. Bates, under Homelessness Research & Action Collaborative. Statewide; filings, notice causes, representation, defaults, stipulations, outcomes, racial disparities. ACTIVE ("Updated on: June 15, 2026"). https://evictedinoregon.com/ ; https://www.pdx.edu/homelessness/evicted-oregon
- Georgia: (a) Metro/Atlanta Region Eviction Tracker — ARC Research & Innovation; credits Eviction Lab, Fulton Co, orig. dev team GA Tech (Elora Raymond, Subhro Guhathakurta), Atlanta Fed, Neighborhood Nexus, Assemblage. 5 core counties. https://atlregional.github.io/eviction-tracker/ (b) Southeastern Evictions Data Collective — GA Tech Planning+Property Lab / CSPAV; Macon, Atlanta, Savannah; address-level + APIs; CURRENCY CAUTION (newest ~2021; portal refused connections 7/14/2026). https://sites.gatech.edu/planningproperty/research-areas-2/southeastern-evictions-data-collective/ (c) EDRN 2026-28 cohort includes GA statewide (lead: Georgia Community Action Association; announcement notes statewide filing data currently unavailable in GA). No "Evicted in Georgia" project exists.
- EDRN — **Eviction Data Response Network**, New America Future of Land and Housing. Launch blog Oct 9, 2024 (inaugural cohort: nine states/regions/jurisdictions, 2024-25; recap names Columbus OH, Louisville KY). June 1, 2026: 11 statewide teams for 2026-2028 — Colorado, Delaware, Georgia, Indiana, Maryland, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, Washington ("representing 26 million renters"); "$3.1 Million Investment From the Ballmer Group" + Pritzker Children's Initiative support. TA + funding + peer learning to "build comprehensive eviction data systems." https://www.newamerica.org/projects/eviction-data-response-network/ ; launch: .../new-america-announces-the-launch-of-its-eviction-data-response-network/ ; 2026: https://www.newamerica.org/insights/state-partners-join-eviction-data-response-network/
- Eviction Lab (Princeton) — national filings database (50 states + DC, 2000-2018) + Eviction Tracking System (monthly, 42 cities + 10 states); ~3.6M filings/typical year. Active. https://evictionlab.org/ [PAGE TREATMENT: one neutral line]

## Cityscape citation (verified from PDF)
Thomas, Tim, Alex Ramiller, Cheng Ren, and Ott Toomet. 2024. "Toward a National Eviction Data Collection Strategy Using Natural Language Processing." Cityscape 26(1): 241-259. HUD PD&R. https://www.huduser.gov/portal/periodicals/cityscape/vol26num1/ch12.pdf (JSTOR 48766080). Abstract verbatim: "...developed a natural language processing (NLP) approach to mine court record images to enumerate and map eviction filing counts at the neighborhood level and help researchers identify disparities by location, race, and gender." Conclusion p. 253 verbatim: "A recommendation to build a more comprehensive national eviction dataset consists of continuing the practice of collecting structured data where available and then gathering court records for text mining in regions with lesser-known trends." + "The upside is that a comprehensive database now seems like a realistic goal."
