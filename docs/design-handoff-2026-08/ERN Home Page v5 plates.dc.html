And what works.

No single lever stops an eviction — it takes four. The four P's, drawn from the profiles and the research.

Open What works →

01 · Prevent

Stop the filing before it reaches court — rental assistance, diversion, pre-filing mediation.

02 · Protect

Tenants once they're in it — counsel, just-cause, and records sealed so they stop following people.

03 · Preserve

The low-cost homes that already exist — housing of last resort is lost quietly, one sale at a time.

04 · Produce

More homes, especially below market — the slowest lever, and the only one that ends the shortage.

Research for social good

We create things that can't be unseen.

Our goal is to expand public & scholarly knowledge on the prevalence & drivers of eviction in under-studied regions. ERN is a research program led by Dr. Tim Thomas in the UC Berkeley Department of Sociology, its academic home.

→Community knowledge is the ground truth; models answer to the people who live there.

→Every product ships with a plain-language summary and its limits in writing.

→Code and data are public wherever the law and our agreements allow.
