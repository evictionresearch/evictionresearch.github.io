State Eviction Timeframes: How Fast Each State's Eviction Clock Runs

2026
Eviction Research Network
/
Report

Statutory eviction clocks for all 50 states and D.C., rebuilt element-by-element from each state's current statutes and court rules and verified against primary law. The fastest lawful nonpayment eviction runs 5 to 56 days depending on the state — and 34 jurisdictions set no ceiling at all. Sortable by stage, with per-element citations, effective dates, and a downloadable dataset.
