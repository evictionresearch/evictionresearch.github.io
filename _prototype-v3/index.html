The Eviction Research Network builds the apparatus people who fight evictions depend on. We do not write to tenants. We build the data, the models, and the methods that researchers, agencies, and courts use.

ERN · Form 01

What this is.

Plain language.

For researchers, agencies, and courts.

ERN is a research network, not a research center. It is housed at UC Berkeley Sociology and confederated across twenty-seven partner organizations — universities, legal-aid offices, county agencies, and media. It is plural by design.

We build state-level eviction data pipelines. We publish open-source methodologies with DOIs and replication code. We steward flagship products like the Housing Precarity Risk Model and the Displacement Typologies, which originated at the Urban Displacement Project and now travel under our care.

We are not a publication. We are not an advocacy organization. We are the infrastructure underneath them.
