# Primer fact-check — CLAIM 8 (representation/RTC), delivered by peer session 2026-07-14

(8a) "4% of tenants vs 84% of landlords have lawyers" — CONFIRMED. NCCRC "Eviction representation statistics for landlords and tenants absent special intervention," last modified March 2026; AVERAGES row "4% 84%" (PDF p.3). URLs: http://civilrighttocounsel.org/highlighted_work/organizing_around_right_to_counsel/ ; http://civilrighttocounsel.org/uploaded_files/280/Landlord_and_tenant_eviction_rep_stats__NCCRC_.pdf
CAVEAT for page: it is NCCRC's AVERAGE across ~40 local/state studies, pre-RTC baselines ("absent special intervention"); local ranges 0-20% / 59-100%. Phrase as "an NCCRC synthesis of local studies (2026)."

(8b) "84% of represented tenants stayed in their homes" (NYC) — CONFIRMED. NYC Office of Civil Justice (HRA), "Universal Access to Legal Services: Year Two," Fall 2019, p.6 verbatim ("84% of households represented in court by lawyers were able to remain in their homes"). https://www.nyc.gov/assets/hra/downloads/pdf/services/civiljustice/OCJ_UA_Annual_Report_2019.pdf
CAVEAT: originated Year One (2018), held FY2019; later years vary (FY2020 86%, FY2022 78%, FY2023 84%, FY2024 72-93% by borough). DATE the figure on the page.

(8c) "roughly five states and about two dozen cities" (2026) — CONFIRMED EXACTLY. NCCRC homepage: "As of 2026, 28 jurisdictions"; "21 cities, 2 counties, 5 states." States: WA (2021), MD (2021), CT (2021), MN (2023), NE (2024). Enacted-legislation PDF "Last modified July 2026": https://civilrighttocounsel.org/uploaded_files/283/RTC_Enacted_Legislation_in_Eviction_Proceedings_FINAL.pdf
CAVEAT: Delaware (2023) + Cincinnati (2023) are "Eviction Defense Programs," NOT RTC — never say six states.

(8d) "$12 for every $1 spent" — CONFIRMED, PHILADELPHIA-SPECIFIC: Stout Risius Ross for the Philadelphia Bar Association, "Economic Return on Investment of Providing Counsel in Philadelphia Eviction Cases for Low-Income Tenants," released 11/13/2018; $12.74:$1 ($3.5M/yr vs $45.2M/yr benefit). URLs: https://legalaidresearch.org/2020/01/30/economic-return-on-investment-of-providing-counsel-in-philadelphia-eviction-cases-for-low-income-tenants/ ; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stout-completes-costbenefit-study-on-right-to-counsel-for-low-income-tenants-facing-eviction-in-philadelphia-300752155.html
CAVEAT: a PROJECTION of avoided city costs, not measured post-implementation return — phrase "a 2018 Stout analysis for Philadelphia estimated $12.74 returned per $1."
