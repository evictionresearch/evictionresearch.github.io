Research for social good

What we do.

The Eviction Research Network collects, analyzes, and maps eviction data while helping other researchers do the same. We specialize in using social and data science to analyze racial and gender disparities in eviction — and urban theory to help explain our findings.

Our goal is to expand public and scholarly knowledge on the prevalence and drivers of evictions in under-studied regions, highlight work from scholars in the field, and provide evidence-based research that legislatures, practitioners, and advocates can use to inform policy.

Partner with ERN

Working on eviction where the data is thin?

We collect and analyze court records, build data pipelines, train collaborators, and co-author research with courts, legal aid organizations, agencies, advocates, and universities. If you have records no one has analyzed — or need help with the ones you have — get in touch.
