§ 01

What ERN is.

The Eviction Research Network is a distributed research infrastructure for housing precarity, displacement, and the eviction system. We build state-level data pipelines, peer-reviewed methodologies, and open-source tools — and we steward them across a network of researchers, agencies, advocates, and courts.

We do not address tenants directly. We build the apparatus that the people who help tenants — legal aid, advocates, county officials, federal policy researchers — depend on.

Methods of record. Code on GitHub. Citations with DOIs. Lineage from the Urban Displacement Project at UC Berkeley to ERN and CiDR Lab.
