§ 01 · about

What ERN is, and what it is not.

Contact · evictions@berkeley.edu

The Eviction Research Network is a distributed research infrastructure. It is housed at UC Berkeley Sociology and confederated across twenty-seven partner organizations — universities, legal-aid offices, county agencies, and media. It is plural by design.

We build state-level eviction data pipelines. We publish open-source methodologies with DOIs and replication code. We steward flagship products like the Housing Precarity Risk Model and the Displacement Typologies, which originated at the Urban Displacement Project and now travel under our care.

We are not a publication. We are not an advocacy organization. We are the infrastructure underneath them.
